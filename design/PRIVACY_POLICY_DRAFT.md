## Information we collect

- **Account information:** an account identifier, email address supplied by Apple or Google, display name, username, profile biography, avatar, and sign-in provider information.
- **Location:** your device location is used to rank nearby places and determine whether location-restricted content may be displayed. A location you choose when publishing a spot—including its coordinates, address, city, and neighborhood—is stored and may be visible to the audience you select.
- **Content and photos:** spot names, descriptions, categories, tags, ratings, reports, Circle content, and photos you choose to upload.
- **Activity:** likes, saved/visited places, preference selections, Circle membership and invitations, blocks, and other actions needed to provide and protect the service.
- **Support communications:** information you include when contacting us.

GetOut does not sell personal information, use personal information for third-party advertising, or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## How we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts; provide profiles, nearby discovery, saved spots, publishing, sharing, and safety features; personalize recommendations; prevent abuse; investigate reports; provide support; and comply with legal obligations.

## Service providers and disclosures

GetOut uses Supabase to provide authentication, database, and file storage services. Apple and Google process information when you use their sign-in services. Device platform services may process requests for location, maps, and address lookup. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business transfer where permitted by law.

## Public and shared content

Public profiles and public spots, including the selected spot location and uploaded content, can be viewed by other people. Circle-only content is available to current members of the selected Circle. Do not publish a home address, another person's personal information, or content you do not have permission to share.

## Retention and deletion

We keep account information and content while your account is active. You can delete your account in **Profile → Settings → Delete Profile & GetOut Data**. Account deletion removes your profile, uploaded spot content and photos, saved places, ratings, social data, and authentication record, except information we must retain for legal, fraud-prevention, or safety reasons. You may also request deletion at [PUBLIC ACCOUNT-DELETION WEB URL].

You can revoke device permissions in system settings and disconnect Apple or Google sign-in through the applicable provider. Removing a device permission prevents future access but does not delete information you previously chose to publish.

## Security and international processing

We use encrypted network connections and access controls designed to protect information. No system is completely secure. Information may be processed in countries outside your own, subject to appropriate legal protections where required.

## Children

GetOut is not directed to children under 13, or the higher minimum age required to consent to data processing where they live. Cannabis-related content is hidden unless the user confirms legal age and the device is in an eligible jurisdiction.
